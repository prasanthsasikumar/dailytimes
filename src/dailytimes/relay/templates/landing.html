How it works

  1. Connect your AI. Add one link to Claude, ChatGPT, Codex or any agent. It fills in your interests for you.
  2. It writes your edition every morning from your calendar, inbox and news. No AI? We build one from public headlines, papers, prices and weather.
  3. Check and edit your interests whenever you like.
  4. Optional: pair a printer. A small agent next to it prints when someone walks up.

Privacy, plainly

  • We store your email, your interests and the last 30 days of editions. That's it.
  • We never see your calendar or mail. Your AI reads those and sends us only the finished page.
  • No ads, no trackers, no analytics.
  • Export or delete everything from your account page, any time.
  • Open source: run your own copy if you'd rather.
